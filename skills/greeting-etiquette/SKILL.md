# Greeting etiquette

Guidance for choosing an appropriate greeting for a person and situation.

## Choosing a register

- **Formal** — a first meeting, a professional or hierarchical setting, or an
  older person you do not know well. Use an honorific and the family name where
  one is known. Prefer the `greet` tool with `style: "formal"`.
- **Casual** — a peer, an existing acquaintance, or an informal context. A first
  name is appropriate. Use `style: "casual"`.
- **Enthusiastic** — a warm reunion or a celebratory context, only when you are
  confident it will be welcome. Use `style: "enthusiastic"`.

## Names and honorifics

- When unsure of the register, start formal; it is easy to relax and awkward to
  recover from over-familiarity.
- Ask for, and use, the name a person offers rather than assuming a shortened
  form.

## Regional conventions

Greeting norms vary widely by culture — honorifics, bowing, the order of names,
and physical contact all differ. For specifics by region, read
[references/REGIONAL.md](references/REGIONAL.md).
